Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 11 August 2026  ·  App: NeuralOS (package com.vortexkode.neuralos)  ·  Contact: vortexkode24@gmail.com

This page explains how to permanently delete your NeuralOS account and the data associated with it. It works whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app (fastest)

If you still have NeuralOS installed and can sign in:

  1. Open NeuralOS and sign in to the account you want to delete
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone
  4. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm, and confirm again

This immediately removes your account and starts deletion of your synced data — see "What gets deleted" below.

Option 2 — Request deletion here (no app or access needed)

If you've uninstalled the app, lost access to your account, or would simply rather not sign back in, request deletion by email instead:

Request Account Deletion by Email →

That link opens a pre-filled email to vortexkode24@gmail.com. Just fill in the account email and send — no account access or password needed. We verify the request matches an existing account and confirm by email once it's done, within 30 days.

What gets deleted, and what's retained

DataWhat happens
Account (email, sign-in method, profile)Deleted immediately from active systems
Synced focus sessions, brain/streak history, achievementsDeleted immediately from active systems
Encrypted backup copiesAge out automatically within 30 days — this is a safety window against accidental/malicious deletion requests, not data we keep on purpose
Support emails you've sent usKept for our own records unless you separately ask us to delete those too
Data that was only ever stored on your own device (offline use)Not something we hold at all — removed the moment you uninstall the app or clear its data yourself
Note: if you have an active NeuralOS Pro subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel billing with Google Play or the App Store — cancel it separately from your store account (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions), or ask us and we'll point you to the right place.
